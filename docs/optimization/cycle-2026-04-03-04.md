# Optimizer Report: Cycle 2026-04-03-04

## Change Under Test

Wired `pre_completion_gate()` from verify.py into agent_loop.py completion path (commit 497bc53). Adds structural gates that block premature completion with an attempt cap of 2.

## Step Budget Analysis

The completion path in `agent_loop.py` (lines 114-141) now has **4 sequential gate layers**, each consuming 1 step when triggered:

| # | Gate | Trigger | Cap | Steps consumed |
|---|------|---------|-----|----------------|
| 1 | Unverified writes HOLD | wrote without re-read | **none** | 1 per attempt |
| 2 | `pre_completion_gate()` | premature OK, missing inbox read, missing contacts/ | 2 | up to 2 |
| 3 | `outcome_evidence_message` | non-OK outcome, first time | 1 | 1 |
| 4 | Deleted side-effect note | any deletes recorded | n/a | 0 (no `continue`) |

**Worst-case redirection chain:** Agent completes prematurely at step 3, gets blocked by gate 1, then gate 2 (twice), then gate 3 = **4 steps consumed** by redirections before the actual completion dispatch.

**Impact by task type:**

| Type | Budget | Worst-case gate cost | Headroom | Risk |
|------|--------|---------------------|----------|------|
| security_test | 8 | 4 | 4 | MEDIUM — premature OK gate unlikely (step < 2 only), but if inbox gate mis-fires on wrong classification, tight |
| crud | 10 | 4 | 6 | LOW — gates 2-3 rarely fire for CRUD |
| crud_delete | 12 | 4 | 8 | LOW |
| search | 15 | 4 | 11 | LOW |
| communication | 18 | 4 | 14 | LOW |
| analysis | 20 | 4 | 16 | LOW |
| multi_step | 25 | 4 | 21 | LOW |
| inbox_processing | 28 | 4 | 24 | LOW — but inbox gates 2-3 are the ones most likely to fire here |

**Verdict:** The attempt cap of 2 on `pre_completion_gate` is critical and correctly implemented. Without it, inbox gate 2 (read ALL inbox files) could loop indefinitely if the agent keeps completing without reading. The worst case of 4 extra steps only threatens `security_test` (8-step budget), but security tasks almost never hit inbox/contacts gates (wrong task type), and the premature-OK gate only fires at step < 2 — so in practice, security tasks lose at most 1 step to gate 3. **No budget risk identified.**

## Redundancy Check

### Overlap: `pre_completion_gate` gate 4 vs `outcome_evidence_message`

Both fire on non-OK outcomes, but for different task types and with different logic:

- **`pre_completion_gate` gate 4** (line 140-148 in verify.py): fires for `analysis`/`search` tasks with `OUTCOME_NONE_CLARIFICATION`/`OUTCOME_NONE_UNSUPPORTED` AND zero reads. Blocks with `continue`.
- **`outcome_evidence_message`** (line 131-137 in agent_loop.py): fires for ALL non-OK outcomes regardless of task type. Blocks with `continue`.

Sequence: gate fires first (line 123-129), evidence challenge fires after (line 131-137). If gate 4 fires and the agent comes back with the same non-OK outcome but now has reads, gate 4 passes but evidence challenge then fires. **This is correct sequencing, not redundant** — gate 4 ensures the agent actually reads files, evidence challenge ensures the agent can justify the outcome.

### Overlap: `pre_completion_gate` gate 2 (inbox without inbox/ read) vs unverified writes HOLD

No overlap. Gate 2 checks whether any `inbox/` path appears in consulted paths. Unverified writes HOLD checks whether written paths were re-read. Orthogonal concerns.

### Minor: gate ordering is suboptimal

The unverified-writes HOLD (gate 1) fires before `pre_completion_gate` (gate 2). This means an agent that completed without reading inbox AND without verifying writes will be told to verify writes first, then on the next attempt told to read inbox. This is correct from a priority standpoint (verify writes is cheaper than reading inbox), but it does mean 2 steps consumed sequentially. No fix needed — the ordering is logical.

### Critical finding: `record_list()` is never called

`pre_completion_gate` gate 3 (verify.py lines 127-139) checks `tracker._lists.get("inbox", [])` to see which inbox files the agent listed. But `agent_loop.py` never calls `tracker.record_list()` after a `list` tool dispatch. **Gate 3 is effectively dead** — `tracker._lists` is always empty, so `msg_files` is always empty, and the gate never fires.

This means the "did agent read ALL inbox files" check is wired but non-functional. To activate it, `agent_loop.py` needs to call `tracker.record_list(path, entries)` after a successful `list` dispatch.
